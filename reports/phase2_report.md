## Notes generated from the numbers above

- Ridge alpha 10000 is at the upper edge of the grid. As alpha grows, Ridge shrinks every coefficient toward 0 and its forecast tends to the training mean.
- FNN early stopping picked a median of 2 epoch(s): validation loss stopped improving almost at once.
- Forecast std on test: Ridge 0.00097, FNN ensemble 0.00137, versus 0.03821 for actual returns. Both models output a nearly constant small drift.
